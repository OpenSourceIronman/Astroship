# Astroship Kickstarter Script

The average Kickstarter video is around two minutes, and a slow professional narration is ~125 words/min. Thus this script is ~250 words long, with a 6 second title card and 9 second end card.

## Script v2026.1

Runtime ~2:00 using ~235 spoken words at a slow professional read (~115–125 wpm)
Read at a calm, unhurried pace. Leave a beat of silence at each section break.
The final end card holds 9 seconds over music, no voiceover.

[0:00–0:06] HOOK — Hero gameplay montage, music swells
"Some rivalries never die. Astroship was built for the iPhone Duo — a multiplayer grid-based battle & puzzle game for friends, family, and enemies."

[0:06–0:41] THE GAME — Gameplay montage: placing fleets, calling shots, hits
"You place your fleet on the space themed grid, call your shots, and explode your opponent before they find you. Sharpen your aim in solo play — then take it online. The Astroship Pro reward unlocks multiplayer and every level once the Test Flight beta is over (May 2027), so you can challenge anyone, anywhere through Apple Game Center. And every solo victory lets you invite your friends straight into the fight."

[0:41–1:11] WHY IT'S DIFFERENT — Theme unlocks, accessibility settings, device lineup
"Fresh looks every three levels — unlock new themes as you climb, from cdeep-space charts to desert battlegrounds. Colorblind-friendly from day one. Timer levels with radar punishments keep every match on a knife's edge. Coming to iPhone and Apple TV, but optimized for the iPhone Duo: one screen for your fleet, one for the hunt. And one rule we'll never break: no ads. Ever. Not on the grid, not anywhere."

[1:11–1:36] WHY KICKSTARTER — Roadmap graphic, beta footage
"The TestFlight beta is live today just visit www.astroship.store/beta, and we launch on the official app to the Apple App Store TODO 2027 three weeks after the end of the Kickstarter. We're raising $42,069 to get there as quick as possible with more themes, more levels, and the polish a tiny independent team can only buy with your help. At official launch TODO 2027 the game itself will be free, and one dollar unlocks Astroship Pro, forever. Starting in Jan 2028, we may switch to a $8 per year subscription if user feedback supports that business decision."

[1:36–1:51] THE ASK — Reward tiers on screen, end card with campaign URL
"Back the campaign and you're in at the ground floor: Astroship Pro with an "exclusive backer offer code" giving you every future theme, and your name in the credits. Help us finish the fleet — please back Astroship today."

[1:51–2:00] END CARD - QR Code and www.astroship.store/kickstarter link to share on social media
"Please share this QR code and astroship.store/kickstarter link with everyone you plan to beat in Astroship"


The price ladder is your best urgency beat. $1 for Astroship Pro now, $4.69 per iPhone after the Kickstarter, $8/year from Jan 2028. "Back today or pay five times more later" writes itself.
"Anywhere on or above Earth" — that's the PRD's actual line for head-to-head play over 0.5 Mbps Wi-Fi or cellular. It's fun, it's authentic, and it belongs in the video.


## Fill in these 8 blanks

1. **ONE-LINE PITCH** — genre + core fantasy, ~12 words
2. **CORE LOOP** — what the player actually does minute to minute
3. **STANDOUT FEATURE** — the headline system
4. **THE THING NO OTHER GAME DOES** — the differentiator
5. **LAUNCH LINE** — platform(s) + date
6. **TEAM / TIMEFRAME / BY HAND** — who's making it, how long, what makes it crafted
7. **WHAT THE $42K FUNDS** — 2–3 specific things the money buys
8. **SIGNATURE REWARD** — the headline backer perk

## Timing notes

- Keep ~2 seconds of silence at each section break — it makes the read feel confident, not rushed.
- Do not promise TestFlight access as a reward (Apple bars it for crowdfunding); "exclusive backer offer code at launch" is the safe phrasing.
