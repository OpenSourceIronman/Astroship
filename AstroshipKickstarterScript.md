# Astroship Kickstarter Script

The average Kickstarter video is around two minutes, and a slow professional narration is ~125 words/min. Thus this script is ~250 words long, with a 6 second title card and 9 second end card.

## Script v2026.0

**Runtime ~2:00 using ~240 spoken words · slow professional read (~125 wpm)**

> Read at a calm, unhurried pace. Leave a beat of silence at each section break.
> The final end card holds 9 seconds over music, no voiceover.

**[0:00–0:15] HOOK** — *Hero gameplay montage, music swells*

"Look up at the night sky and ask yourself: what if I could go? Astroship is our answer — [ONE-LINE PITCH: genre + core fantasy, ~12 words]."

**[0:15–0:50] THE GAME** — *Gameplay systems montage*

"In Astroship, you [CORE LOOP: what the player does minute to minute]. [STANDOUT FEATURE: the headline system]. And [THE THING NO OTHER GAME DOES: your differentiator]. [LAUNCH LINE: e.g. 'It launches October 26th on iPhone.']"

**[0:50–1:20] WHY IT MATTERS** — *Dev footage, concept art, the team at work*

"We're [TEAM: who you are, e.g. 'a tiny independent team led by Clifton Evans'], and we've spent [TIMEFRAME] building this [BY HAND: what makes it crafted, not factory-made]. This isn't a factory game. It's the game we always wanted to play — and now it's almost finished."

**[1:20–1:45] WHY KICKSTARTER** — *Roadmap graphic, feature checklist*

"The beta is in final review right now. Your backing takes Astroship from a great launch to a great game: [WHAT THE $42K FUNDS: 2–3 specific things]. Every dollar goes into the game you see on screen."

**[1:45–2:00] THE ASK** — *Reward tiers on screen, end card with campaign URL*

"Back the campaign and you'll get Astroship at launch with an exclusive backer offer code, plus [SIGNATURE REWARD: the headline backer perk]. Help us finish the ship — back Astroship today."

---

## Fill in these 8 blanks

1. **ONE-LINE PITCH** — genre + core fantasy, ~12 words
2. **CORE LOOP** — what the player actually does minute to minute
3. **STANDOUT FEATURE** — the headline system
4. **THE THING NO OTHER GAME DOES** — the differentiator
5. **LAUNCH LINE** — platform(s) + date
6. **TEAM / TIMEFRAME / BY HAND** — who's making it, how long, what makes it crafted
7. **WHAT THE $42K FUNDS** — 2–3 specific things the money buys
8. **SIGNATURE REWARD** — the headline backer perk

## Timing notes

- At 120–130 wpm the filled-in script lands at ~1:55–2:05. Read it aloud twice and trim or stretch to taste.
- Keep ~2 seconds of silence at each section break — it makes the read feel confident, not rushed.
- Do not promise TestFlight access as a reward (Apple bars it for crowdfunding); "exclusive backer offer code at launch" is the safe phrasing.
